# Complaints and Appeals

## Complaints

A complaint is acknowledged within 5 business days. A complaint is resolved within 30 calendar days. Unresolved complaints are escalated to the Complaints Review Committee. Customers can contact the state insurance regulator at any time.

## Claim Denial Appeals

A claim denial appeal must be filed within 60 days of the denial letter. The appeal decision is made within 30 days. The appeal is reviewed by a claims manager who was not involved in the original decision. A second-level review by the claims committee is available if the first appeal is denied.
