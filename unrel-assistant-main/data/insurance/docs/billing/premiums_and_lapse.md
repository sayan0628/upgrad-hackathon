# Premiums, Lapse and Refunds

## Payments and Fees

Premiums can be paid monthly, quarterly, semi-annually or annually. The autopay discount is 3% on auto and home policies. A late fee of $10 applies to payments received more than 10 days after the due date. A returned payment fee of $25 applies when a payment is rejected by the bank.

## Grace Period and Lapse

The grace period is 31 days for life policies and 30 days for health policies. Coverage stays in force during the grace period, and a claim for a loss in that period is paid with the unpaid premium deducted. Auto and home policies have no grace period, and Acme Mutual sends a 10-day written notice before cancelling for non-payment. A policy lapses when the grace period ends without payment.

## Reinstatement

Life policies can be reinstated within 24 months after the lapse date. Reinstatement requires evidence of insurability, all unpaid premiums and interest at 6% per year. Health policies cannot be reinstated, and the customer must apply again during open enrollment or a special enrollment period. Auto policies can be reinstated within 30 days after cancellation if the past-due premium is paid, otherwise coverage has a gap.

## Refunds and Free Look

When Acme Mutual cancels a policy the refund is pro-rata. When the customer cancels an auto or home policy mid-term the refund is 90% of the unearned premium. Term and Whole Life policies have a 30-day free look period with a full refund. Individual health policies have a 10-day free look period. Travel plans have a 14-day free look period if no trip has started and no claim has been filed.
