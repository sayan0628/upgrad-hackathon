# Travel Insurance Guide

## Plans and Limits

Acme Travel offers three plans: Essential, Plus and Premier. Trip cancellation reimburses up to $5,000 on Essential, $10,000 on Plus and $25,000 on Premier. Emergency medical expense coverage is $50,000 on Essential, $100,000 on Plus and $250,000 on Premier. Emergency evacuation coverage is $250,000 on Essential, $500,000 on Plus and $1,000,000 on Premier. Baggage delay benefits begin after a delay of 12 hours. The maximum trip length is 90 days.

## Conditions and Upgrades

The pre-existing condition exclusion is waived if the plan is bought within 14 days of the first trip deposit. The Cancel For Any Reason upgrade reimburses 75% of the prepaid, non-refundable trip cost if the trip is cancelled at least 48 hours before departure. The Cancel For Any Reason upgrade must also be bought within 14 days of the first trip deposit. Covered cancellation reasons include illness, death of a family member and jury duty.
