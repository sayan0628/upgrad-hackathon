# Individual Health Insurance Guide

## Plan Tiers and Cost Sharing

Acme Health Individual offers four plan tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum. The annual deductible is $7,000 on Bronze, $4,500 on Silver, $2,000 on Gold and $500 on Platinum. The annual out-of-pocket maximum is $9,100 on Bronze, $8,000 on Silver, $6,000 on Gold and $3,500 on Platinum. The primary care visit copay is $40 on Bronze, $30 on Silver, $20 on Gold and $10 on Platinum. The specialist visit copay is $80 on Bronze, $60 on Silver, $40 on Gold and $25 on Platinum. The emergency room copay is $500 on Bronze, $400 on Silver, $300 on Gold and $150 on Platinum. The generic drug copay is $15 on Bronze, $12 on Silver, $10 on Gold and $5 on Platinum.

## Coverage Rules

Dependent children are covered until they turn 26. Maternity coverage has a 10-month waiting period on Bronze and Silver and no waiting period on Gold and Platinum. Prior authorization is required for MRI, CT and PET scans and for non-emergency inpatient admissions. Telehealth visits have a $0 copay on Gold and Platinum. Out-of-network care is covered at 50% coinsurance after the deductible on Gold and Platinum. Bronze and Silver do not cover out-of-network care except in an emergency. One annual wellness visit is covered at 100% on every tier.

## Enrollment

Open enrollment runs from November 1 to January 15. A special enrollment period of 60 days starts after a qualifying event such as marriage, birth of a child or loss of other coverage. Coverage starts on the first day of the month after the application is approved and the first premium is paid.
