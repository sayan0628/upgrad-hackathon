# Whole Life Insurance Guide

## Overview

Acme Mutual Whole Life provides lifetime coverage with level premiums and a guaranteed cash value. The issue age range is 18 to 75 for adult policies. Juvenile Whole Life policies can be issued for children from 30 days old to 17 years old. The minimum face amount is $25,000 and the maximum face amount is $5,000,000. The policy is fully paid up at age 100.

## Cash Value and Loans

Cash value grows at a guaranteed minimum rate of 2% per year. Policy loans are available once cash value exists, up to 90% of the cash value. Policy loans carry a fixed interest rate of 6% per year. An unpaid loan balance is deducted from the death benefit. Dividends are not guaranteed and are declared annually by the board.

## Surrender

Surrender charges start at 10% of the cash value in policy year 1 and decline by one percentage point each year until they reach zero after year 10. Surrender requests must be made in writing and the payout is made within 30 days. Surrendering the policy ends all coverage.
