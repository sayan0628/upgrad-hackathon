# Auto Insurance Guide

## Coverages and Deductibles

Acme Auto offers liability, collision, comprehensive, uninsured motorist, medical payments, rental reimbursement and roadside assistance coverage. The lowest liability limits offered are 25/50/25, meaning $25,000 per person, $50,000 per accident and $25,000 for property damage. Collision and comprehensive deductible options are $250, $500, $1,000 and $2,000. Windshield repair has no deductible. Rental reimbursement pays $40 per day for up to 30 days. A vehicle is a total loss when the repair cost is 75% or more of its actual cash value.

## Discounts and Surcharges

The good student discount is 10% for full-time students under age 25 with a grade point average of at least 3.0. The multi-policy discount is 15% when the customer also holds a home or life policy with Acme Mutual. The telematics discount is up to 20% after 90 days of driving data has been collected. The paid-in-full discount is 5% when the annual premium is paid in a single payment. The combined discounts, including the autopay discount, cannot exceed 30% of the base premium. Drivers under age 25 carry a 25% surcharge. An at-fault accident adds a 20% surcharge for three years. The minimum age for a named driver is 16.
