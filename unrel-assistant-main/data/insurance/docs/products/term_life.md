# Term Life Insurance Guide

## Overview

Acme Mutual Term Life provides level-premium death benefit protection for a fixed term of 10, 20 or 30 years. The issue age range is 18 to 65 for the 10-year term, 18 to 60 for the 20-year term and 18 to 50 for the 30-year term. The minimum face amount is $50,000 and the maximum face amount is $2,000,000. Premiums are level for the entire term and cannot increase during the term.

## Underwriting and Rating

Medical exam requirements for Term Life follow the Underwriting Guidelines. An applicant is a tobacco user if they have used any tobacco or nicotine product in the last 12 months. Tobacco users are charged 200% of the non-tobacco premium.

## Contestability and Exclusions

The contestability period is two years from the policy issue date. During the contestability period Acme Mutual may investigate the application and rescind coverage for a material misstatement. The suicide exclusion applies for two years after the issue date and limits the benefit to a refund of the premiums paid.

## Riders and Conversion

The Accelerated Death Benefit rider is included at no extra charge and allows up to 50% of the face amount to be paid early if the insured has a terminal illness with a life expectancy of 12 months or less. The Waiver of Premium rider pays the premiums if the insured is totally disabled for more than six months. The Accidental Death rider doubles the death benefit when death results from an accident. A Term Life policy can be converted to Whole Life without a medical exam until the end of policy year 10 or until the insured reaches age 65, whichever comes first.
