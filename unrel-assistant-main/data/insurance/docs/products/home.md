# Home Insurance Guide

## Coverage Limits

Acme Home pays for damage to the dwelling up to the dwelling limit chosen by the customer. Other structures coverage is 10% of the dwelling limit. Personal property coverage is 50% of the dwelling limit. Loss of use coverage is 20% of the dwelling limit. Liability limits of $100,000, $300,000 or $500,000 are available. Medical payments to others coverage is $1,000 to $5,000.

## Deductibles and Settlement

Flat deductible options are $1,000 and $2,500, and wind and hail deductibles can be 1% or 2% of the dwelling limit. Roofs older than 15 years are settled at actual cash value, and newer roofs are settled at replacement cost. Jewelry and watches are limited to $1,500 in total unless they are scheduled on the policy.

## Exclusions and Riders

Flood and earthquake damage are excluded and require separate policies. The Water Backup rider adds $5,000 of coverage by default and can be raised to $25,000. Coverage is restricted after the home has been vacant for 60 consecutive days.
