# Agent Conduct Standards

## Agent Obligations

Agents must hold a licence in the state where the customer lives. Agents disclose their commission on request. A replacement notice must be given at least 10 days before an existing policy is replaced. Agents cannot accept cash payments. Agents forward customer complaints to Acme Mutual within 2 business days. Agents complete 24 hours of continuing education every two years. Agents cannot promise dividends.
