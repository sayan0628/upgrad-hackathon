# Privacy and Data Retention Policy

## Retention Periods

Policy records are kept for 10 years after the policy ends. Claim files are kept for 7 years after the claim is closed. Records of declined applications are kept for 3 years. Call recordings are kept for 3 years. Copies of identity documents are kept for 7 years after the policy ends or the claim closes, whichever is later. Juvenile policy records are kept until the insured turns 25 or for 10 years after the policy ends, whichever is later.

## Customer Rights

Data access requests are answered within 30 days. Deletion requests are honoured unless the record is covered by a retention period. Marketing opt-outs take effect within 10 business days. Affected customers are notified of a data breach within 60 days.

## Data Protection Controls

Access to personal data is limited by role. Personal data must not be sent to external services without redaction.
