# Age Verification and Identity Policy

## Scope and Deadlines

Acme Mutual verifies the date of birth of every applicant and insured before issuing a life, health or travel policy. Age verification must be completed within 30 days of application submission or the application is closed as incomplete. Applicants under age 18 cannot own a policy, and a parent or legal guardian must apply as the policy owner. The minimum age for a primary health applicant is 18.

## Accepted Documents

Accepted proof of age documents are a government-issued photo ID, a passport, a certified birth certificate, a permanent resident card and a military ID. Documents must be unexpired, except that a certified birth certificate has no expiry date. A driver's license that expired less than 12 months ago is accepted as proof of age but not as proof of identity. Social media profiles, utility bills and school IDs are not accepted as proof of age. If the name on the document differs from the application, the applicant must provide a marriage certificate or court order. Juvenile applications require a certified birth certificate.

## Digital Verification

Digital ID verification through an approved vendor is accepted for applications with a face amount of $500,000 or less. Applications above $500,000 require a certified document copy or in-person verification. Copies of identity documents are stored encrypted.

## Claims and Age Misstatement

For life insurance claims, the age of the insured is re-verified against the death certificate. If the age was misstated, the death benefit is adjusted to the amount the premiums paid would have purchased at the correct age. Any payout above $10,000 requires identity re-verification of the payee.
