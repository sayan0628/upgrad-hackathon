# Life Underwriting Guidelines

## Risk Classes

Acme Mutual rates life applicants in five risk classes: Preferred Plus, Preferred, Standard Plus, Standard and Substandard. Each table rating for a Substandard applicant adds 25% to the Standard premium.

## Exam Requirements

Applicants aged 18 to 45 need a paramedical exam for face amounts above $250,000. Applicants aged 46 to 60 need a paramedical exam for face amounts above $100,000. Applicants aged 61 and older need a paramedical exam for any face amount. Applications above $1,000,000 also require a financial questionnaire.

## Decisions

Applications without an exam receive a decision within 5 business days. Applications that require an exam receive a decision within 15 business days after the exam results arrive. Declined applicants receive a written notice of the reason within 10 business days.
