# Claim Processing Guide

## Reporting Deadlines

Auto and home claims must be reported within 30 days of the loss. Travel claims must be reported within 60 days of the loss. Life claims should be reported as soon as reasonably possible after the death. Health claims follow the Health Claims Guide.

## Process Steps

Step 1 is the first notice of loss, where the customer submits the claim form. Step 2 is an acknowledgement sent within 2 business days. Step 3 is the assignment of an adjuster within 3 business days. Step 4 is the collection of documents and, when needed, an inspection or investigation. Step 5 is the coverage decision, made within 30 calendar days after all required documents are received. Step 6 is payment, made within 5 business days after approval by direct deposit or within 10 business days by check.

## Required Documents

Every claim requires a completed claim form and proof of loss. Theft claims and collision claims with injuries require a police report. Repairs above $2,500 require two repair estimates. If the claim payout is above $10,000, the payee must pass identity re-verification before payment.

## Approval Authority and Timing

Adjusters can approve claims up to $25,000. Claims from $25,001 to $100,000 require senior adjuster approval. Claims above $100,000 require claims committee approval. When Acme Mutual requests more information, the 30-day decision clock pauses until the information is received. Interest of 6% per year is paid on undisputed amounts that are not paid within 30 days after the decision.

## Denials

A denial is communicated in writing, with the reason, within 5 business days of the decision. The customer can appeal a denial within 60 days of the denial letter.
