# Fraud Review and Special Investigations

## Red Flags

Red flags include a loss within 60 days of the policy start date, three or more claims within 12 months, inconsistent statements and altered documents. A claim with two or more red flags must be referred to the Special Investigations Unit.

## Investigation

The Special Investigations Unit completes its investigation within 45 days of the referral. While a claim is under investigation, the customer receives a written status update every 30 days. A claim referred to the Special Investigations Unit is paid only after the unit clears it. Confirmed fraud is reported to the state fraud bureau within 30 days of the determination. Acme Mutual may deny a claim that contains a material misrepresentation.
