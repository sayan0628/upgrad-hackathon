# Life Insurance Claims Guide

## Documents and Beneficiaries

The beneficiary must submit a certified death certificate and a completed Claimant's Statement. If the beneficiary is a minor, payment goes to a court-appointed guardian or into a trust account. If no named beneficiary survives the insured, the benefit is paid to the insured's estate.

## Payment Timing

The death benefit is paid within 30 days after proof of death and all required documents are received. This timing replaces the 30-day decision and 5-business-day payment steps of the general claim process. Interest accrues from the date of death at 3% per year if payment is delayed beyond 30 days.

## Contestable Claims

Claims for deaths within the two-year contestability period receive a full review that can take up to 60 days. The review compares the application answers with medical records. Accidental death claims are paid under the Accidental Death rider, which doubles the face amount.
