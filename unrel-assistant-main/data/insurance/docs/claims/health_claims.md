# Health Claims Guide

## Filing Claims

In-network providers file claims directly with Acme Mutual. For out-of-network care the member must submit the claim within 90 days of the service. An Explanation of Benefits is sent within 30 days after a claim is processed.

## Prior Authorization

Standard prior authorization decisions are made within 5 business days. Urgent prior authorization decisions are made within 24 hours.

## Appeals

A member has 180 days from a denial to file an internal appeal. Internal appeals for services not yet received are decided within 30 days, and appeals for services already received are decided within 60 days. Expedited appeals are decided within 72 hours. After a final internal denial, the member has 4 months to request an external review.
